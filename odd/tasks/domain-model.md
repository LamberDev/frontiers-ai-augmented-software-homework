# Feature: domain-model

## Objective
Domain model for the peer-review backend: shared kernel (`Entity<TId>`, `Error`, `ErrorType`,
`ValidationError`, `Result`, `Result<T>`), `User` and `University` entities with their
invariants, the reviewer eligibility rule as pure domain logic, persistence ports in
Application and their EF Core InMemory implementation in Infrastructure.

## Why
`RegisterUser` and `InviteReviewer` (later steps) need a validated domain, an eligibility rule
and persistence. This step builds those foundations without use cases, HTTP client or endpoints.

## Scope
- In: `PeerReview.Domain` (SharedKernel, Users, Universities, Reviewers), `PeerReview.Application`
  `Abstractions/Persistence` ports, `PeerReview.Infrastructure` persistence + `AddInfrastructure()`,
  `PeerReview.Domain.UnitTests`, new `PeerReview.Infrastructure.IntegrationTests` (in `PeerReview.slnx`),
  `apps/api/AGENTS.md` *Tests* section.
- Out: handlers/use cases, Frontiers HTTP client and DTO, endpoints, OpenAPI, calling
  `AddInfrastructure()` from `Program.cs`, frontend, Docker.

## Constraints and decisions (confirmed by the user, 2026-09-25)
- Ids: `Guid` generated by the domain with `Guid.CreateVersion7()`; EF `ValueGeneratedNever()`.
  Deliberate deviation from the brief's `InviteReviewer(int UserId)`: ids are not enumerable,
  do not leak the user count, are known before persisting and do not depend on the data provider.
- `FrontiersOrganizationId` (`long`, `> 0`) is a business key, not the identity of `University`.
  Users of the same university share one `University`.
- `UserName`: required, trimmed, 1..100 chars after trimming. Not unique.
- `NumberOfPublications`: `>= 0`, no upper bound.
- `University.Name`: required, trimmed, max 250 chars. Empty `organizationName` handling belongs to
  the anti-corruption layer (later step; rejected there, `matchedName` never used).
- `Score`: `decimal?` (null = unknown), negative is invalid, no upper bound. It is probably the
  Elasticsearch relevance score; the brief ("Score >= 60") is applied as-is (README note).
- Score snapshot: stored at first registration of a university; `University` has no update method.
- Factories accumulate all validation errors into one `ValidationError : Error` with an `Errors` list;
  `Result` keeps one `Error`.
- Ineligibility is a successful outcome (`IsEligible = false` + reasons), not an `Error`:
  `IneligibilityReason(Code, Message)` record, catalogue in `Reviewers/ReviewerIneligibilityReasons.cs`.
- Messages: short English sentences ending with a period, including the limit when useful.
- The InMemory provider does NOT enforce the unique index on `FrontiersOrganizationId`; the
  no-duplicates guarantee comes with the get-or-create handler.
- Commits: user consented to one commit per work unit on `feat/domain-model`; no push.
